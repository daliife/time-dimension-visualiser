How to read the cube

  • The bright sheet is the current frame (Time).
  • Faint sheets are other moments; Gap separates them in space.
  • Rotate to see pixel trails on the cube faces (motion through time).
  • The 3D time ruler on the front edge shows seconds (0–4) like a measuring tape.

The app stacks ~72 frames of a short clip: width and height stay on each sheet, and the third axis is time. Normal playback is a cut moving one frame at a time; orbiting changes the angle of that cut. Try Reset view, raise Gap, then scrub Time or press Play.
